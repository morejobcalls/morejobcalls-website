What You're Buying

Contractors Leads Service: What You're Actually Paying For

Every contractors leads service sells a version of the same thing, so ask what happens after the homeowner raises their hand. That's where the money gets made or lost.

  1. Who else can buy this homeowner? If the answer isn't "nobody", you're in a price race before you've said hello.
  2. Who calls them, and how fast? A lead that waits until tomorrow is usually gone. Ask who owns the follow-up.
  3. What's checked before it reaches you? Project type, timing, budget, service area. Without that, you drive to tire kickers.
  4. Who owns the asset? Some vendors rank one website and rent you the calls. When you stop paying, the source stops too. Ask whose ad account and whose data it is.
  5. What happens if it doesn't work? "We'll replace bad leads" is not the same as a written guarantee with a number and a remedy.

Our answers: nobody else, the system follows up for you, the application qualifies them, and you own your ad accounts and your CRM data from day one, so you're never held hostage. The guarantee is below.
