## Why this order

Ranked by *leverage per hour*, not search volume:

1. **Glossary** — 0% built, cheapest to write, and definition pages are what answer
   engines quote verbatim. Every term below currently has **zero** occurrences
   site-wide, on a site whose entire thesis is "judge marketing on cost per
   appointment, not cost per lead." We argue the metric and don't define it anywhere.
2. **Appointments** — 5 case studies, 0 evergreen how-tos. These are the exact
   commercial-intent terms the business sells against. "appointment setting" appears
   on 7 non-deck trade pages and **not on the deck pillar**.
3. **Pillar economic spine** — the definitive page has 4,092 words and zero instances
   of "CPL" or "cost per lead".
4. **Meta sub-cluster** — one 5,459-word page carrying a 16-target cluster.
5. **Lead-gen holes** — the brand's core argument ("exclusive vs shared") has no page.
6. **Geo** — uncontested, but only after the national layer holds.
