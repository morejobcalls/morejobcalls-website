The Guy in Your Sales Call Is the Guy Running Your Ads

More Job Calls is operator-led on purpose. No account managers, no fresh grad with a checklist, no outsourced media buyer. That's not a limitation — it's the product.

Who We Are

More Job Calls (legally SeasonProof Growth LLC) builds done-for-you lead generation systems for home service contractors — deck builders, roofers, fence, siding, window, pool, landscaping, patio cover, and pergola companies across the United States.

Founded and run by Spencer Wright, the company works with one contractor per market per trade and backs its work with a written guarantee: 100 exclusive sales appointment opportunities in 100 days, or a full management-fee refund plus $2,000 cash.

Why This Exists

Built for Contractors Who've Been Burned

Almost every contractor we work with says some version of the same thing on the first call: "I've had six or seven agencies. Same pitch, same slide deck, same promises. Then a bill and a voicemail I'll never get returned."

More Job Calls was built as the answer to that. It started in the decking niche (as DeckingLeads.com), where the system was proven on deck builders doing $1M–$3M a year, and grew into the trades that share the same buyer: homeowners making big-ticket decisions about the outside of their home.

Three structural choices make it different, and none of them are marketing copy:

  1. You own everything from day one. Ad account, CRM, data, funnels, creatives. You pay Meta directly. If you walk, you walk with the whole engine. No hostage situations.
  2. One contractor per market, per trade. When your territory is claimed, it's locked. Your leads are never resold to a competitor — that's the entire point.
  3. The guarantee has real money behind it. 100 exclusive sales appointment opportunities in 100 days, or every dollar of your management fee back plus $2,000 cash. We'd rather pay than apologize.

And one more thing most agencies won't offer: the reference roster. Past clients have agreed to take live phone calls from on-the-fence prospects. Not a testimonial video — an unscripted call with a builder who was exactly where you are now.
