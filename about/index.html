Who We Are

MoreJobCalls.com is a marketing company for deck builders and other home-service contractors that books exclusive sales appointments onto the owner's calendar instead of selling shared leads. It works with deck, patio cover, pergola, fence, pool, siding, window, landscaping and roofing companies across the United States. The legal entity is MoreJobCalls.com LLC.

Founded and run by Spencer Wright, the company never sells the same appointment to two contractors, and it backs its work with a written guarantee: 100 exclusive appointment opportunities in 100 days, or you get paid three ways: a 100% refund, a $2,000 check from us, and we keep working for free until we hit 100.

Why This Exists

Built for Contractors Who've Been Burned

Almost every contractor we work with says some version of the same thing on the first call: "I've had six or seven agencies. Same pitch, same slide deck, same promises. Then a bill and a voicemail I'll never get returned."

More Job Calls was built as the answer to that. It started in the decking niche (as DeckingLeads.com), where the system was proven on deck builders doing $1M–$3M a year, and grew into the trades that share the same buyer: homeowners making big-ticket decisions about the outside of their home.

Three structural choices make it different, and none of them are marketing copy:

  1. You own your accounts and data from day one. Ad account, leads, customer data. You pay Meta directly. The CRM, funnels, and creative are our proprietary system, licensed to you while we work together. No hostage situations.
  2. Every appointment is yours alone. It's never resold to a competitor. That's the entire point. If you want your whole market locked, exclusive territory is available on our VIP tier.
  3. The guarantee has real money behind it. 100 exclusive appointment opportunities in 100 days, or you get paid three ways: a 100% refund, a $2,000 check from us, and we keep working for free until we hit 100. We'd rather pay than apologize.

And one more thing most agencies won't offer: the reference roster. Past clients have agreed to take live phone calls from on-the-fence prospects. Not a testimonial video — an unscripted call with a builder who was exactly where you are now.
