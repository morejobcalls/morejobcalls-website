How This Works

The system is the same in every trade: Meta ads built on proof-heavy creative, a multi-step qualification funnel that filters for project type, budget, timing, and decision-makers, and CRM automation that turns leads into sat appointments. What changes per trade is the economics — ticket size, season, and what a junk lead looks like.
