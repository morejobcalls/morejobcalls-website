How It Runs

SEO Company for Contractors: How the Work Runs

  1. Audit first. We fix what's broken on the site you have before building anything new.
  2. Map the searches. A keyword map of what your buyers search, sorted by what you can win soonest.
  3. Build the money pages. Service and city pages first, because they're what a hiring homeowner lands on.
  4. Fix the map results. Google Business Profile, citations and reviews, so you show up where local trades get chosen.
  5. Publish and link. Every page indexed and wired into the rest of the site.
  6. Report on appointments. We track which calls and forms came from search, and how many became appointments.

AI Search

Getting AI to Recommend You by Name

More homeowners now ask ChatGPT or Google's AI who to hire before they ever open a list of websites. When the answer names your company, that's about as close to a referral as marketing gets, and buyers tend to trust it the way they'd trust a friend's recommendation.

Those answers are built from what the web already says about you: your pages, your reviews, your profile and the sites that mention you. So the same work that ranks you on Google is what gets you named. We build it on purpose, then test the questions your buyers ask to see whether you come up.

General Contractors

SEO for General Contractors

A general contractor sells a wide range of projects, so the site has to show Google, and the homeowner, exactly which ones you want more of. That means a page for each project type you actually sell, pages for the areas you serve, and a profile that lists every service separately instead of one vague category.

It also means being choosy. The map should chase the remodels and additions you want, not every small job that happens to have search volume. A GC site that ranks for handyman work fills the phone with the wrong calls.

Roofing

SEO for Roofing Contractors

Roofing search is crowded and price-driven, and storm seasons bring out companies that disappear afterward. Homeowners know it, so reviews, real job photos and a profile that's clearly been around for years carry extra weight. Residential replacement and commercial roofing are different buyers with different searches, so they get different pages.

FAQ

Contractor SEO Agency: Common Questions

What does a contractor SEO agency actually do?

It gets your business found by homeowners who are already searching for your trade, on Google, Google Maps and AI answers. For us that means an audit and fixes, a keyword map, the service and city pages that map calls for, your Google Business Profile, citations, a review engine and the internal linking that ties it together. Then we measure it the way you do: booked appointments, not rankings.

How much do contractor SEO services cost?

We don't publish a price, because it depends on your market, how many cities you serve and what your site needs fixed first. You get the real number on a 30-minute strategy call, along with a straight answer on whether SEO should come first for you or later.

How long does contractor SEO take to work?

Plan on six to twelve months before SEO produces steady appointments, and longer in a competitive metro. That's why we're honest about pairing it with paid demand when your calendar needs filling sooner.
