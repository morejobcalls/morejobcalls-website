Frequently asked questions

What is More Job Calls?
A done-for-you appointment system for deck, patio cover, pergola, fence, pool, siding, window, and roofing companies. We build the ads, the landing page, the application, the follow-up automations, and the calendar inside your own accounts, then we run it. The customer-facing name for the system is The Deck Jobs System.
Why should a deck builder choose More Job Calls over a local agency?
Local agencies sell proximity. We sell a system that has already been installed in 100+ contractor accounts since 2024, with $100M+ in client-booked jobs behind it. The market intelligence comes from data, not zip codes: your best customer profile, your competitors' pricing, your top-converting project types, and the call recordings that tell us what your buyers actually say.
How fast will I see results?
Build takes 3 to 7 days. First opportunities typically land within 72 hours of launch. Chris Walters had 13 to 14 appointments a week within his first month. Derek Lopez signed $46,000 in his first 30 days. Your numbers depend on your market and budget, and we'll give you an honest projection on the call.
What if the appointments are junk?
We don't sell shared, recycled leads. These are exclusive, pre-qualified appointment opportunities. The application filters for project type, budget, timing, and decision-maker before anything hits your calendar, and the follow-up sequence warms them up so they show. Are some tire-kickers going to slip through? Occasionally. That's why the guarantee is on 100 opportunities, and why we keep testing toward the ads that produce closed jobs, not cheap clicks.
Do you share appointments with other contractors?
No. Every appointment is 100% exclusive to your business. Exclusive territory ("one builder per market") is available on our VIP tier; ask about it on the call.
I've been burned by agencies before. Why is this different?
That's exactly why this offer exists. Most of our clients came in skeptical. Brian Wallace told us on his first call that he didn't want to learn about the system, he wanted to call our bluff. Six weeks after starting: 60 deck appointments and 25 closed jobs, more than all of the previous year. We tie our own neck to a number, 100 exclusive opportunities in 100 days or we write you a $10,000 check, so you don't have to trust words. You can trust the structure.
Am I locked into a contract? Do you keep my accounts?
No lock-in, no hostage situations. It's a 100-day sprint, and you own the ad account, CRM, data, funnels, and creative from day one. If we stop working together, nothing breaks. The engine stays in your business.
Is this just lead gen?
No. Lead gen gives you a name and a phone number that four other contractors also paid for. We give you a qualified homeowner who owns the property, has a real project, picked a date and time, and got reminded so they actually show up. The ads, application, follow-up, and calendar all run before it ever touches you.
What does it cost, and who is it for?
Pricing depends on the sprint and your ad budget; we walk through it on the strategy call, not on a pricing page. It's built for established companies doing $500K a year or more with crews and someone who can run 3 to 5 appointments a day. If you're still swinging the hammer yourself, this will overwhelm you, and we'll tell you that on the call.
