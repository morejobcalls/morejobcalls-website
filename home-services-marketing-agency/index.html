Emergency calls and planned projects need different marketing

Same-day service work is won by being visible and answering first: the map pack, Local Services Ads, a phone that never goes to voicemail. That's what most home services marketing companies are tuned for, and they're good at it.

A $15,000 fence or a full roof replacement sells differently. The homeowner plans for weeks, wants to meet the owner, and the price gets set at the table. That buyer needs to trust you before you arrive, be qualified before they book, and get followed up with when life gets in the way. That's the part our system was built for.

The System

How Our Home Services Marketing Works

Build in 3 to 7 days, launch, then improve it every week from what actually closes:

  1. Pick the jobs worth chasing. Which project types, job sizes and neighborhoods you want more of, and which ones you'd happily never see again.
  2. Put your face on it. The ads feature the person who'll be at the door. Homeowners have already met you by the time you knock.
  3. Qualify before the calendar. An application with real questions, then a booking calendar. Serious homeowners finish it. Window shoppers mostly don't.
  4. Follow up automatically. Anyone who applies and doesn't book gets worked by text and email. Anyone who books gets reminded.
  5. Trace every closed job. Each signed contract gets tied back to the ad that started it, and the budget moves toward what closes.
  6. One team, start to finish. Spencer and team run your account directly, on a shared board with regular calls. No handoff to someone you've never met.

Who owns what

You own your ad accounts and your CRM data from day one, so you're never held hostage. Everything is built inside your own accounts, so nobody can hold a login over you.

What the first week looks like

An onboarding call, a 3 to 7 day build, then launch. First appointments land within 7 days of launch. Derek L. had 2 appointments booked within 6 hours of his.

What the system does not do

It doesn't close the job for you. The system controls the appointments, and the guarantee covers them. The closing is yours, and we coach it.

Choosing a Partner

Home Services Marketing Company or Lead Seller? How to Tell

Search this phrase and you'll find agencies, lead sellers and directories all using the same words. Here's how to tell what you're actually buying.

Seven questions to ask before you sign

  1. What do they report every month? Rankings and clicks, or booked appointments and closed jobs?
  2. Do you own the accounts? Your ad accounts and your customer data should be in your name, in writing.
  3. Is each homeowner yours alone? If the same homeowner gets sold to four companies, you're in a price war before you've said hello.
  4. Can you see results from your kind of trade? Named owners, real numbers, on video. Not a wall of five-star quotes with no names.
  5. What happens if they miss? A guarantee needs a number, a deadline and a consequence.
  6. Who runs your account after you sign? Ask for the name.
  7. Do they know how big jobs sell? Spouses at the table, weeks of planning, pricing on site. If every answer is about call volume, they're built for a different business.
