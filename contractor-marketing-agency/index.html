Choosing a Partner

Best Marketing for Contractors: How to Judge an Agency

Most contractors who call us have been burned before. So before you sign with anyone, us included, run them through this list.

The checklist

  1. What are they measured on? If the report is impressions and rankings, that's what they're optimizing. You want booked appointments and closed jobs.
  2. Who owns the accounts? The ad accounts and your customer data should be yours. Get it in writing.
  3. Are the appointments exclusive? If anyone else can buy the same homeowner, you're racing on price.
  4. Can you talk to contractors like you? Not a testimonial page. Named results, on video, in your trade.
  5. What's the guarantee, and what's the remedy? "We'll make it right" isn't a guarantee. A number, a deadline and a consequence is.
  6. Who actually runs your account? The person on the sales call, or someone you meet after you pay?
  7. Do they understand how a high-ticket job sells? Spouses at the table, weeks of planning, pricing on-site. If they only talk about clicks, keep looking.
